## Production rule
A figure may be promoted to ACCEPT only when its provenance matches the claim made by the frozen caption. Source-specific empirical claims were replaced with source-backed reproductions, public-data experiments, controlled numerical experiments, or explicitly labeled scientific reconstructions where the original evidence was unavailable. No reconstruction is presented as an original dataset, benchmark, clinical, or simulator result.
