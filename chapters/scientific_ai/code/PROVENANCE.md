# Figure-generation provenance

Figures 2–5, 7, 9, 10, and 12 are deterministic Matplotlib scientific renderings constructed from explicit mathematical or systems representations; they intentionally contain no fabricated empirical metrics. Their canonical vector outputs are committed in `../figures/`.

Figure 6 uses RDKit Morgan fingerprints (radius 2, 2048 bits), public molecular structures, and experimentally reported Stokes et al. antibiotic activity. The core quantitative computation is in `figure_06_drug_discovery.py`.

Figure 8 is fully reproducible from scikit-learn's Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset using `figure_08_wdbc_baselines.py`; exact fold-level results are committed in `../data/figure_08_wdbc_cv_results.csv`.

Figure 11 is generated by training the PINN in `figure_11_pinn_residual.py` on the 1-D heat equation and evaluating both the automatic-differentiation residual field and the field error against the independent analytical reference.

Figure 1 is the current raster rendering from the image-generation production pass and therefore has no executable plotting script. It should be treated separately from the code-rendered quantitative figures.
