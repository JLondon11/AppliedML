# Figure 1 — Scaling Laws Across Model Size, Data, and Compute

Chapter: NLP Part II

Panels (a) and (b) evaluate the published Kaplan et al. (2020) fit
`L(N,D)=[(Nc/N)^(alpha_N/alpha_D)+Dc/D]^alpha_D`, with
`alpha_N=0.076`, `alpha_D=0.103`, `Nc=6.4e13`, and `Dc=1.8e13`.

Panel (c) evaluates the Hoffmann et al. (2022) fit
`L(N,D)=1.69+406.4/N^0.34+410.7/D^0.28` along its compute-optimal
frontier under `C≈6ND`. It is a derived curve from published fitted
parameters, not raw empirical observations.

No empirical points, uncertainty bands, or repeated-run statistics are fabricated.

Sources: Kaplan et al., arXiv:2001.08361; Hoffmann et al., arXiv:2203.15556.
