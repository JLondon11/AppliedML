## Generator semantic gate

Generic deterministic fallback plots are not final publication evidence when a frozen caption requires an exact method, dataset, benchmark, simulator, or empirical computation. Such figures remain blocked until generated by a figure-specific implementation or sourced from verified data.

## Release gate

No chapter passes figure QA until every final render has been inspected at publication size and reaches ACCEPT after:
1. provenance verification;
2. best-scientific-alternative review;
3. quantitative colorbar/legend validation where applicable;
4. redundancy review;
5. panel/caption correspondence review;
6. typography, spacing, and whitespace review.
