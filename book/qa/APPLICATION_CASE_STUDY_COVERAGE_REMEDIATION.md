# Application and Case Study Coverage Remediation Summary

Source baseline: AppliedML Complete Book Proof Pass20.

Hard rule: every Application and every Case Study in the publication-ready manuscript must:
1. begin with multiple substantive introductory/background paragraphs;
2. then use bold standalone structured labels with full prose after each;
3. include at least one scientifically appropriate figure tied directly to that section;
4. include at least one benchmark-comparison table tied directly to that section;
5. use real/cited/reproduced numerical evidence rather than invented illustrative results.
