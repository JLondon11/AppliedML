## Hard interpretation

A chapter is **not safely stored** until its root editable `.tex`, compiled PDF, complete retained tri-format figure set, full listing code, manifests/checksums, and final ZIP are committed in GitHub.

Local/runtime recovery sources remain useful as input, but they do not count as preserved until promoted into this repository.
